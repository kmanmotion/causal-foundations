# Lean 4 verification supplement

The [Lean project](lean/README.md) formalizes Theorems 2, 3 and 13-16 and the
deterministic branch of Theorem 1 of the published v1.0 paper. It also contains
Theorem 4's finite structural foundations; its stochastic genesis endpoint remains
unfinished. Verification includes compilation, axiom audits and fresh kernel replay.
The [coverage inventory](lean/THEOREM_COVERAGE.md) distinguishes these completed
endpoints from the remaining paper. See [GitHub Actions](https://github.com/kmanmotion/causal-foundations/actions/workflows/lean.yml)
for the actual verification result of each commit.
